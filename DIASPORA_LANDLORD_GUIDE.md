# Locari PM OS — Diaspora & Remote Landlord Guide 🌍

A foundational pillar of **Locari Property Management Operating System** is bridging the **trust and communication gap** for overseas property owners.

---

## 1. The Core Challenge

A large percentage of Nigerian real estate is owned by Nigerians living abroad in the **United Kingdom, United States, Canada, Europe, South Africa, and the UAE**, or professionals residing in different Nigerian states.

### Traditional Frustrations:
* Complete dependence on informal agents, relatives, or acquaintances.
* Inability to verify whether a tenant actually paid rent on time.
* Fabricated maintenance invoices and inflated contractor costs.
* Difficulty obtaining clear, consolidated financial statements.
* Time-zone mismatches and expensive international communication.

---

## 2. How Locari Solves It

### A. WhatsApp as the Remote Command Cockpit
* Diaspora landlords do not need to install complex desktop software or remember passwords.
* They simply communicate with the Locari Assistant on WhatsApp using their international phone numbers (e.g. `+44...`, `+1...`).
* The system detects their timezone (e.g. `Europe/London`, `America/New_York`) and delivers formatted summaries in Naira (with exchange rate clarity when needed).

### B. Transparent Expense Authorizations
* When a repair exceeds the threshold (e.g. ₦50,000 for a borehole pump replacement), the system sends an authorization card to the landlord on WhatsApp with contractor diagnostic notes and photos/videos.
* The landlord simply replies: `"Approve repair"` to authorize the work.

### C. Automated Monthly Remittance Statements
* At the close of each month, the system calculates:
  $$\text{Net Payout} = \text{Total Rent Collected} - \text{Agency Management Commission} - \text{Approved Repairs}$$
* The landlord receives a clean breakdown on WhatsApp showing the amount transferred to their Nigerian NUBAN bank account (e.g. GTBank or Zenith Bank).

### D. 24/7 Access to the Web Control Centre
* Whenever the landlord wants a bird's-eye view of all their compounds on a laptop, they reply `"Dashboard"` on WhatsApp.
* The bot sends a secure **Magic Link** that logs them directly into their web dashboard without requiring a password.
